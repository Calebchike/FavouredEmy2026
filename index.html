Making your way

Both venues are in Woji. Open the directions link on each event to plan your route from where you are staying.

The Solemnization of Holy Matrimony begins at 10:00 AM on Saturday 12th December 2026, West Africa Time.

Watchman Catholic Charismatic Renewal Movement, Port Harcourt Diocese, Blossoming District, at 4B Samson Chukwu Estate, by Zacozem Filling Station, Alcon Road, Woji, Port Harcourt.
